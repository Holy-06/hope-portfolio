My Superpower

The Strategist

I like to understand a problem before I touch it. I break it into pieces, look for the simplest path, and plan before I build. Strategy isn't about having all the answers — it's about asking the right questions and staying flexible when things change.

  • Break problems down, then prioritise
  • Plan the path, but stay ready to adapt
  • Keep solutions simple and maintainable

The Builder

This is where I get hands-on with cloud and DevOps — AWS, Linux, Docker, CI/CD and Git. I enjoy the craft of making things run reliably: automating the boring parts, shipping cleanly, and understanding what happens behind the scenes.

  • Cloud infrastructure & networking
  • Containers, CI/CD and automation
  • Version control with Git & GitHub

The Connector

Technology is better when it brings people together. Through event strategy, social media and community work, I've learned that the human side matters — clear communication, looking after guests, and creating space for people to contribute.

  • Event strategy & guest management
  • Social media & community engagement
  • Volunteering and on-the-day coordination

The Learner

I'm a student in the best sense — curious and always adding to the toolkit. New tools, new ideas, and hard problems are exciting, not intimidating. I'd rather understand something deeply than move on too quickly.

  • Curiosity over comfort
  • Learn the why, not just the how
  • Turn new skills into real projects
